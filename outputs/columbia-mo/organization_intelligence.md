## SECTION 2 narrative

### Governance Body
The City Council serves as the primary governing body with capital responsibilities. The Housing and Community Development Commission and Parks And Recreation Commission also have governance roles for their respective functional areas.

### Executive
The City Manager's Office holds executive capital responsibilities. Department heads including Police, Fire, Fire Marshall's Office, Community Development, Parks and Recreation, Public Works, Public Works Administration, and Utilities report to this executive structure.

### Finance/Procurement
Finance responsibilities are distributed across multiple entities: Accounting & Finance, Finance, and Finance Department. Procurement responsibilities are handled by Contracting & Purchasing, Purchasing, and Purchasing Division, with the latter two having separate procurement functions.

### Public Works/Utilities/Facilities
Public Works and Public Works Administration manage construction capital projects. Utilities and Stormwater Engineering handle utility-related capital projects. Fire and Fire Marshall's Office manage fire department facilities.

### CIP Project Approval and Procurement Process
The approval process for CIP projects is not confirmed in researched sources. Procurement appears to be centralized through Contracting & Purchasing, Purchasing, and Purchasing Division, with separate procurement functions for the latter two entities. The specific workflow for how projects move from initiation to approval to procurement is unknown based on the provided evidence.
